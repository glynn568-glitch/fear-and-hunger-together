**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.20.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs the same version.

New:

- **Instanced loot.** A host option, in Together on the title screen or in Options > General during a run: each of you finds your own loot in every container and corpse, instead of whoever gets there first taking it. It is off unless the host turns it on. Everyone sees the host's choice in Together, and is told when the host changes it.
- **Ready picks your character.** Ready (or Join, for a run under way) opens the character list, and your pick readies you. "Come in as..." is gone.
- **The title screen works with the mouse**, like the Together panel on it.

Fixed:

- **Enemies only one of you could see.** Four enemies came out in one player's game only: the hounds that turn up once you've been on a floor a while, and three others. Your partner saw nothing there, or saw the enemy stand still while it went for you. Now they come out for both of you. Our rule: if one of you can see an enemy, so can the other. The cost is that what one of you does to it happens for both: wake one and it wakes for both of you, and one that leaves when you change rooms now leaves when either of you does. We'd take that over being hit by something you can't see.
- **One enemy counted its hits on both of you together.** Its third hit kills, so one of you could die after fewer hits than the game allows. Each of you now has your own count.
- **Both of you opening the same chest at once** could give you both its loot. With Instanced loot off, the first to open it takes it.
- **A friend whose game loaded slowly could miss the lobby** and see the plain title menu until the host chose a run.
- **Doing the same thing at the same moment.** When both of you changed the same thing in the world at once (both laying a body down, say), each game could keep the other's version for good. Both now agree.

Known issue: if you split up across two floors of one area, an enemy your partner wakes on their floor can wake on yours too.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
