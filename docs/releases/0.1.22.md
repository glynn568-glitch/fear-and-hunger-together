**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.22.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs the same version.

Changed:

- **An AI partner sent home with F8** keeps its body for the rest of the run, with its gear and wounds. Call it back and the same one returns: a living one at your side, a dead one still dead. Before, the next call could bring someone new.

Fixed:

- **Splitting up across two floors of one area**: an enemy your partner woke on their floor could wake on yours too. It now wakes only on its own floor. Walk onto that floor while it's up and you'll see it where your partner does. A door taken on that floor sends it away; a door on the other floor doesn't.
- **Two of you taking in the same companion a moment apart**, such as a skeleton raised with Necromancy or the girl in the cage, could each get one. Now the first of you does, and the other is told.
- **With three or more of you**, a party mate fighting in someone else's fight showed their health and status in your menu as it was before the fight. It now shows them as they are.
- **Coming back under another name** after your game dropped out of a fight left the enemy you were fighting frozen for everyone for five minutes. If you come back in the same browser tab, it's now free as soon as you're back.
- **Two of you playing on the host's PC**: once the first left, the other couldn't see Balance changes or Instanced loot in Options. Now they can.
- **An enemy frozen for good**: if someone's game dropped or reloaded while that enemy was hitting them, it stood still for everyone for the rest of the run. Now it carries on.
- **A scene your partner already set off** could stall the same scene for you: you'd trigger it, the screen kept shaking or the next step never came, and you were stuck where you stood. It now plays out for you too.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
