**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.10.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs this same version (0.1.9 players are asked to update).

Fixed:

- **A boss you have just beaten no longer fights you again.** When a partner won a fight you were in (the Cave Mother), walking into the boss before their victory scene ended started a second fight at full health, and its ending could leave you unable to move. The boss's death now reaches every player before anyone can touch it again.
- **Cave gnomes stay dead for everyone.** A gnome one player killed in the Caverns was still alive for the other. Every other enemy's death was already shared; these were the only ones that were not.
- **Every floor stays the way the run first threw it.** Which version of a floor you get and who stands where on it (the Blood pit's lizardman, the Mines' layout, where D'arce and the others turn up) is decided by dice when a run starts. A player whose game threw them again later changed those floors for everyone. Now the first throw stands.
- **The Caverns lift works for both players.** When one player rode it down, the other player's game played the arrival too: it walked them a step and could leave the cage hanging a tile above its shadow, so nobody could get in.
- **The Salmonsnake no longer takes you to another copy of the Mines.** A slip in the game's own data sent anyone who went back to the snake, on two of the Mines' three layouts, to the first layout's map: away from their partner and into a fight nobody could join.
- **Typed prayers work.** The name box added the letter under its cursor when you pressed Enter or Space, so "O LORD" came out as "O LORD " or "O ALORD" and the scroll did nothing. Spaces at either end are dropped, a key that types a letter only types it, and the scroll's verses ignore capitals.
- **Your partner walks smoothly on your screen.** On a shaky line (Wi-Fi, a Steam Deck) their character used to stop between tiles whenever a message came late. Their steps now play a moment behind, without breaks.
- **Hosting online hands the link out sooner**, usually within 10 to 15 seconds instead of up to 90.
- **A dropped friend notices within seconds** and reconnects, instead of playing on alone for minutes.
- **Chat typed while the connection is down is sent when it comes back**, marked "not sent yet" until then.

Changed:

- **Hunger runs a little slower while you play with another person** (four fifths of the game's rate), since nobody can pause. Alone, or with only the AI partner, it is the game's own.
- **F9 shows your line to the host** (round trip in ms), and the host's log keeps every player's numbers. If it feels slow, press Ctrl+Shift+B after playing a while and send the report: it now says which machine, the line or the fight picture is the slow part.

Good to know: the lift in the Caverns hangs in its shaft if you walked onto that floor. Stand on its shadow below and use the mechanism to ride it.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
