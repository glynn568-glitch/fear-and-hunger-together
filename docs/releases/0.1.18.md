**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.18.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs the same version.

Fixes from a three-player session.

Fixed:

- **Invisible after a lift.** When two players rode a lift a few seconds apart, the second one could arrive invisible and stay that way, and enemies fought someone nobody could see.
- **Enemies closing in while you talk.** Alone, the game stops every enemy while you are in a conversation or a scene. In co-op they kept walking up to you. Now the ones nearest to a player in a scene stand still.

The AI partner:

- **It says when its model stops answering.** A partner whose AI service was out of usage went silent with no word why, and kept asking it every few seconds. Now it tells you once, with the reason, and tries again later. It still follows, fights and takes plain orders.
- **Changing how it thinks takes effect at once.** Saving another choice in the partner panel now sends the partner out and straight back with it. Before, it only counted for the next partner you called in.
- **It comes back for you.** On its way to a ritual circle to call you back it now answers what stands in the way, opens locked doors if it can, and fights through what blocks the path.
