**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.6.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs this same version (0.1.5 players are asked to update).

- **Joining a fight that is already under way no longer gives a black screen.** You see the fight at once, come in at the start of the next turn, and choose your own moves. Before, you saw nothing until the next turn, and if you still could not see when your turn came, the game chose for you.
- **Players whose game files differ no longer get each other's enemies crossed.** On a map where your copies differ, each of you meets your own enemies there; switches, items and progress are still shared, and maps that are the same in both copies work as before.
- **Chat works on the title screen too.**
- Bug reports (Ctrl+Shift+B) now list which game files differ from your partner's, and every fight and join.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
