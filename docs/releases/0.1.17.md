**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.17.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs the same version.

Joining a game in progress works again, and the AI partner is much quieter.

Fixed:

- **Joining a game already under way.** Continuing a save while your partner was already playing could stop halfway: you played on alone, your partner saw you sitting at the title, and nothing either of you did reached the other. This came in with 0.1.15.
- **Moved furniture after a load.** Coming back in from the title could put a slid bookshelf somewhere it never was.
- **Locked out of your own game.** After a join that went wrong, the host could not load their own save until everyone restarted. A player whose game is back at the title no longer holds the run.
- **A join that fails says so** on screen, instead of leaving you playing alone without knowing.

The AI partner:

- **It answers only when you say its name** whenever two or more people are playing. Before, it took far too much of your chat as meant for it.
- **It talks much less.** Fewer remarks, no running commentary in fights, no greeting every time it joins, and "quiet" now covers fights too.
- **Pick who it plays.** **Plays as** in the partner panel: D'arce, Cahara, Enki or Ragnvaldr. If someone already plays that one, it takes a free one.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
