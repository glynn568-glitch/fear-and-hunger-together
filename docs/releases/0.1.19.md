**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.19.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs the same version.

Starting a night together is now one step for everyone.

New:

- **Ready up at the title.** With two or more of you at the title screen, the host picks the run: a new one, or one of their saves. Everyone else presses Ready, and the host's Start takes everybody in at the same moment. Nobody has to load in the right order any more.
- **Pick your character before the game starts.** On a new run each of you chooses who to play at the title, and one that is taken is greyed out. The opening then starts you as that character.
- **Continue finds your save.** When the host continues a save, your own save from that night is found for you. You no longer have to pick the matching one from the list.
- **Join a run under way.** Arriving late, or coming back after leaving, the title shows Join.
- **The list in the corner says who is here**, and one word for each: ready, not ready, loading, in, away.
- **Away.** Someone who has not touched a key for five minutes at the title is marked away and no longer holds up the start.

Fixed:

- **A dropped connection on the way in.** If your line dropped for a moment during the opening, someone else could take your character and you ended up playing alone. Your place and your character are now kept for you.
- **Changing your name mid-run.** Changing the name in the name box and pressing Continue could bring an older save of yours back into a run you had already been in.

Playing alone is unchanged: New Game and Continue, as before.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
