**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.11.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs this same version (0.1.10 players are asked to update).

Fixed:

- **The AI partner works on a Steam Deck.** It plays in a browser of its own, and it only looked for Chrome where Windows and most Linux PCs keep it. SteamOS installs Chrome as a Flatpak, so pressing F8 said "Chrome or Edge was not found". It now finds Chrome, Chromium, Edge or Brave installed as Flatpaks too. No Claude account is needed: without one, choose "Call it in without AI" and it follows and fights without talking.
- **The AI partner no longer freezes after picking up gear.** Putting on a better weapon or armour made it take everything off and put it back on, over and over. With one spare piece its game slowed to a crawl, and with two or more it locked up and dragged on the host's PC.
- **Pressing Play twice no longer wipes the session's log.** Starting the game again while it was already running moved the running session's log aside, so a bug report sent afterwards came out empty.

Changed:

- **The host's log keeps the last five sessions** and everything each player's game noted (fights joined or sat out, script errors), with the time it happened, even if their connection was down at that moment. A bug report sent after a session now tells the whole story from every player's side.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
