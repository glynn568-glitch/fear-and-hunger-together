**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.12.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs this same version (0.1.11 players are asked to update).

New:

- **The AI partner runs on whatever you have.** Open **How it thinks** in the Together panel and pick Claude Code, Codex (ChatGPT login), Grok Build, Hermes (SuperGrok or Nous Portal), an API key from Anthropic, xAI, OpenAI, Google Gemini or OpenRouter, a model on your own PC in Ollama or LM Studio, or any service that speaks the OpenAI chat API. The model list fills from the service, and the key and model are checked with one short question before anything is kept. Keys stay on your PC. Codex and Grok Build are locked down: they cannot run tools or touch your files, and Codex runs without your own instructions, settings or plugins.
- **The partner without a model is smarter.** It takes orders typed in plain words ("give me a herb", "wait here", "stay close", "what do you have"), says out loud when it is hungry or hurt, and fights the way a careful player does: it guards when an enemy winds up, heals a partner who is close to death, and hits the part that ends the enemy in the fewest blows (a guard's torso, not its head). It needs no subscription.
- **A slow model fights fast.** If its fight turns take more than about 8 seconds, the partner fights by those same rules at once and keeps the model for talking.

Fixed:

- **The game starts from more copies and folders.** A copy whose data files were saved with a byte-order mark (some editors and translation tools do this), a `settings.json` saved from Notepad, or a Windows user folder named in Cyrillic or with accents (José, Иван) could stop the game with "The game could not start". These now start. If the game still cannot start, the window says which file and what was wrong, and where the log is.
- **Coming back after a dropped line works for players with non-English names in their folder path.** Their saved place was never cleared, so a rejoin could put them back where they had been earlier.
- **A controller can use the AI partner panel (F8).** It never could.
- **The AI partner no longer skips a turn after a long round.** If a round ran past 25 seconds (long fight text, or a host taking their time), the partner's next turn was never asked for, and the fight waited until someone chose for it.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
