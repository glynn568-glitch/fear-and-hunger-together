**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.13.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs this same version (0.1.12 players are asked to update).

The AI partner:

- **It looks after itself.** It searches bodies, barrels and chests near its path when it follows you or walks in your party, and eats clean food before it asks you for any. It picks its own goals when you give it none (loot, food, rest, a ritual circle) and walks there. It does not ask you for more.
- **It steps around traps.** Armed bear traps, spike traps, nail boards and arrow plates are walls for the partner and for anyone walking in a party line. A floor that only nibbles a few HP (the bug swarm on level 7) is crossed when the way leads over it.
- **It is called by its character's name** (D'arce, Cahara...). "Claude" and the old name still reach it.
- **Orders from both players count.** "Follow" from the second player makes it follow them. The newest order stands, and it says who it goes with. "Wait" ends when it is attacked or when the one who said it leaves the map.
- **Fight orders reach the fight.** "Guard" or "take the left arm" said in chat is used on its next turn. In a fight, its chat knows the enemy and its parts.
- **It reads tells better.** It guards only where guarding stops the next blow, and otherwise hits the part that is about to strike.
- **Dead is dead.** It says one last line, then stays silent and makes no model calls until it is revived.
- **It only answers when it is talked to.** Talk overheard between players no longer costs a model call each time. "You, grab that" and nicknames reach it; talk about it does not.
- **It stays out of fights with no enemy** (the iron bars).
- **In a party, a fight that starts while the leader is busy with an event waits for the leader** (up to 90 seconds) instead of the partner fighting alone.

Party and items:

- **You can use items on a party mate from the game's Item menu** (food, herbs, cloth, vials). The item is used once, on their machine.
- **Two soul stones carried to a ritual circle revive a partner in the circle's place,** so the circle stays. Not on Hard.

Base game fixes:

- **Weapons do what their descriptions say.** The mace and morning star stun, and the morning star, shark teeth and scimitar make the target bleed, and the ritual spear poisons. Bleeding now lasts 4 turns at 5% a turn. Rates are kept modest.
- **Options > General > Balance changes** (host only, on by default) turns the weapon changes and the soul stone revive on or off.
- **Bear traps in the prisons catch the elite guard.** The game's crossbow wrote its own position over the elite guard's every frame, so a trap never saw the guard (maps 6 and 37). The same fix lets traps catch the skeleton on the Prisons map (172).
- **Silver coins go past 99,** up to 999.
- **The loudest sounds are quieter.** The mace and morning star swing was about 14 dB louder than the median sound.

Fixed:

- **The Equip screen of a party mate is look-only again.** Equip, Clear and Optimize on a mate could take an item from your bag and lose it.
- **Sending a dead partner home (F8) no longer revives it** when it is called back.
- **Fight starts hitch less on weak GPUs (Steam Deck).** Battle pictures stay loaded for 5 minutes instead of being decoded again for every fight.
- **The meter no longer shows a negative fps** for relayed video.
- **Sending the partner home while it loads** no longer prints an error.
