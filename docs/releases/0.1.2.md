**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.2.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs this same version (0.1.1 players are asked to update).

- **Doors open for everyone.** A door one player opens is now open for all players, AI partners included. Before, it only opened on that player's own screen, and the AI partner got stuck at it and teleported after a minute.
- **The AI partner opens doors itself** when one stands in its way, and follows you around corners and through rooms instead of waiting at a door.
- **Missing game, explained.** If Fear & Hunger isn't found, the launcher now says so plainly before asking for its folder, and a folder with a different game in it (Termina, say) is turned down with the reason.
- **A friend without the mod** who opens your link is told where to get it when the button opens nothing.
- **An AI partner that drops out comes back as itself.** After a crash it sometimes came back as a fresh character with empty pockets; now it carries on from where it dropped out.
- The "you have joined a world" notice no longer runs under the player list at the top right.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
