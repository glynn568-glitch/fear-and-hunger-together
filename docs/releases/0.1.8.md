**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.8.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs this same version (0.1.7 players are asked to update).

- **Getting back just after a fight was won no longer undoes it for you.** If your line dropped near the end of someone else's fight and you got back just as it was won, you could come home with your health and items from before the fight. Now you come home with what the fight left you.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
