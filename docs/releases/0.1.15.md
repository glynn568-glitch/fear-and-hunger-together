**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.15.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs the same version.

Fixed:

- **Moved furniture stays moved for everyone.** When one player slid the mockup shelf, the other player got the flags but kept the shelf in the old spot, with no door. Shelves, doors and anything else a story scene moves are now shared, also for someone who arrives later or loads a save. Saves from older versions work.
- **A save carried into a run no longer replays its scene.** Loading a solo save into someone's run could start Trortur's torture of Buckman for everyone and pay the reward again. The save keeps your body and bag; the scene stays in its own run. A recruit you bring along also stays gone from their cage.
- **New Game gives a new run.** When the last person goes back to the title on purpose, the run ends and the AI partner goes with them. It comes back by itself when you start again: its old body if you continue that run, a new one in a new run.
- **The Crow Mauler is one hunt.** He could hunt one player and be invisible to the partner on the same map, and if the partner was simulating that map he never moved. Now you both see him, and he only comes once.
- **A short line drop costs nothing.** Your party line and the partner following you survive a drop of up to 20 seconds. Others see "X is back." A line that dropped at the title no longer put you back in the old run.
- **No more bleeding doors.** Weapon effects do not land on doors and bars.
- **Chat** no longer doubles a line on a controller.

The AI partner:

- **It obeys what was said to it.** "i have to wait for u" is not an order to wait, and a bare "yea" is not an order at all, but a yes to its own question counts. With two people talking, "you" no longer always means the partner.
- **It speaks only of what it holds,** and it knows what it just used on itself.
- **It answers a menu once.** It stopped saying "leave him be" over and over.
- **It does not search the same body again and again.**
- **It comes along when its leader goes into a door or bars fight,** also when the leader is a guest there.
- **It speaks English unless you tell it otherwise,** and one name to a line.

Known: the partner can still slip into another language that uses the Latin alphabet. Lines in other alphabets are dropped.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
