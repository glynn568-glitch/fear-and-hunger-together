**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.4.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs this same version (0.1.3 players are asked to update).

- **Endings play for everyone.** Before, only the player who reached an ending saw it; everyone else stayed in the dungeon, and the one who finished was left as a dead player who could be called back. Now whoever heads into an ending takes the whole party along, the dead included, and the run ends for all of you together.
- The link-box fix from 0.1.3 is in here too, with a wider check behind it: every button and box in Together is now tested to take a real click, at more than one window size.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
