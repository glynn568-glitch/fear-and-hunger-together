**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.16.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs the same version.

This one is the end game: Ma'habre, the last floors and every ending, each played through with two players.

Fixed:

- **No black screen in the Passages of Ma'habre.** A player who got there without reading the book (in a party line, following through a door, the AI partner, a revive) had no way back. When their time ran out, the game sent them to a map that does not exist. Now everyone keeps their own way back, and someone who came in with a player who entered by Ma'habre's own doors gets no timer either.
- **Scenes stay with the player who started them.** The Beacon of the Depths and other scenes that run in steps could play on a partner somewhere else and leave their screen dark for good.
- **The next plane no longer locks up.** If Le'garde took the throne through one player while the other was already on the chair, that player stood there with no menu. The last scenes now play for whoever is there alone.
- **One fight at the god of yourself.** When two players arrived together, the second skipped the fight and went straight to an ending while the first was still fighting. The second now waits and can join the fight.
- **The girl at the pit asks her own player.** A partner standing near could answer her first and take the fight.
- **Everyone gets the same ending, exactly.** A player partway along another ending could get the wrong one, and two players finishing at once could each get a different one. The first ending reached is the one everyone plays.
- **Ending S tells one story:** the story of the player who earned it, for everyone.
- **After the credits.** New Game while your partner was still in the credits joined the finished run. Someone in the credits no longer counts as alive or holds their character.
- **Party mates in the menu stay current.** A status gained or cured while the menu is open now shows, and five status icons fit on a row.

The AI partner:

- **It says when it cures itself.** "Used my Green herb. Infection's gone."
