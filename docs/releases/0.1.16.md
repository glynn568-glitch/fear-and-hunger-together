**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.16.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs the same version.

This one is the late game and every ending, each played through with two players. No spoilers below.

Fixed:

- **No black screen in a late area.** A player who came in with someone else (in a party line, following through a door, the AI partner, a revive) could be sent to a map that does not exist when the area sent them back. Now everyone keeps their own way back.
- **Scenes stay with the player who started them.** Some late scenes could play on a partner somewhere else and leave their screen dark for good.
- **A softlock near the end.** When both players reached one of the last scenes, the second could be left standing there with no menu.
- **One fight, not two.** When two players arrived at a late fight together, the second could skip it and go straight to an ending while the first was still fighting. The second now waits and can join the fight.
- **Questions go to the player they are meant for.** A partner standing near could answer first and take that player's fight.
- **Everyone gets the same ending, exactly.** A player partway along another ending could get the wrong one, and two players finishing at once could each get a different one. The first ending reached is the one everyone plays, and it plays the same for everyone.
- **After the credits.** New Game while your partner was still in the credits joined the finished run. Someone in the credits no longer counts as alive or holds their character.
- **Party mates in the menu stay current.** A status gained or cured while the menu is open now shows, and five status icons fit on a row.

The AI partner:

- **It says when it cures itself.** "Used my Green herb. Infection's gone."

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
