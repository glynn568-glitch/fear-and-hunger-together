**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.9.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs this same version (0.1.8 players are asked to update).

New:

- **Party mode.** Face a partner, press Enter and join their party: you walk in line behind them, go through doors together and fight as one party. The AI partner joins yours if you tell it "join my party".
- **The run goes on without the host.** Everyone keeps their own saves on their own PC, and every bed saves the world for every player, so the others can carry the run on when the host can't make it.
- **Controllers and the Steam Deck.** Every key-only action has a button, the game's own torch and phase included. Together, the AI partner panel and the chat work with the D-pad and A/B, and the prompts name the button once you pick up a pad. On a Steam Deck or Linux, start `app/play.sh` (the README says how to add it to Steam for Game Mode); it opens full screen on SteamOS.
- **Partner arrows.** A small, faint arrow at the edge of the screen points to a partner on the same map who is out of sight. Turn it off under Options > General > Partner Arrows.
- **The AI partner talks like the character it plays**, from that character's own lines in your copy of the game, one short line at a time. It falters when its Mind runs low.
- **Bring your single-player saves in** from Together: they are copied into free slots, once each, and loading one starts a co-op run from that moment.
- **G lays a bear trap.**

Fixed:

- **Scenes play only for the player who started them.** The elevator, the mechanisms, Trortur's table and about 130 more used to move or kill a partner somewhere else on the map, or anyone who walked in later.
- **The hound's leap goes only for the player it was fighting**, as do Trortur's talk-down, Moonless joining and Seril's senses. Before, the hound could maul a partner who never fought it.
- **One fight per enemy.** Two players could each start a fight with the same priests, and a dropped friend's fight could hold an enemy for minutes ("an extra guard spawned").
- **You can hand things to a partner who is in their menu.**
- **Hosting online:** when Cloudflare hands out no link, the panel says so and to try again, instead of quietly going back to "Just you". Stopping and starting hosting at once (to change Hide my IP) works. If your link stops working mid-game, you are told to host again and send the new one.
- A friend who comes back under a new name is told whose name their saves are under.
- Reviving on the map you died on no longer fails now and then.

Known limit: saves from before this version don't record which player started which scene, so a scene from one of those saves can still play for everyone once. New saves are fine.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
