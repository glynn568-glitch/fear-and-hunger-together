**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.7.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs this same version (0.1.6 players are asked to update).

- **A dropped connection no longer wrecks a fight.** If your line drops mid-fight, you stay in your seat and get back in when it returns, still choosing for your own character. Before, you were thrown back to the map, could not rejoin ("already in that party"), and an old result could later overwrite your character and bag.
- **If the player running the fight drops, you wait for them** instead of being thrown out.
- **While you are away, your character fights with your own items,** not the other player's.
- **The enemy being fought stays put while its fighter is offline.** Before, it went after the other player and could start a second fight.
- **The host's game decides the run.** A friend who gets into the dungeon first waits for the host, then joins whatever the host starts or continues. Before, whoever got in first set the run, and a host who then pressed Continue ended up playing alone. The friend's title screen says so.
- **Minimising the game, or covering it with another window, no longer freezes a fight for the others.** Before, a fight's owner checking Discord stopped it for everyone until they came back.
- **Your link is copied once it actually works.** A new link can take Cloudflare a few seconds (on a bad day, minutes) to publish; a friend who pasted it too early was told nobody was hosting. Now the Together panel waits for it, and a friend who is too early is told to try again in a minute.
- **Fight video through the host is real video now:** up to 60 pictures a second on a sixth or less of the upload the old still pictures took, and it eases off on a slow connection instead of falling seconds behind.
- **Hide my IP address is now on by default,** so nobody learns anyone's IP address. Untick it on the Together panel to connect to each other directly.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
