**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.14.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs this same version (0.1.13 players are asked to update).

Fixed:

- **The host's game no longer stops in the Lord of flies fight.** The fight forces a move at the end of some turns. When that move was gone by its turn, the game tried to use it anyway and stopped with an error. Now it is dropped, as the game drops any other move that cannot be used.
- **Your guard counts when you are not the host.** A guard at the right moment makes a grab miss: the guard's charge, the night lurch's leap, the priest's chant, the harvestman's arms and the rest. In a shared fight, only the host's character's guard ever counted. Now any player's guard counts, and it covers the whole party.
- **The walls are right after you follow someone.** Some doors change how a map looks after you go through (the Ancient city). Following a player (F), walking in their party line and being called back at a ritual circle skipped that, so you arrived with the wrong walls. Now you arrive with what they see.
- **Soul stones leave your bag when you call someone back,** and come back if nobody answers. Before, stones handed over during the call could revive for free.

The AI partner:

- **It sees grabs coming.** It guards in the turn a grab lands. When another player already chose to guard that turn, it attacks instead.
- **It stops guarding where a guard does nothing.** Cave gnome bites bleed and rot limbs through a guard, so it charges them one at a time. Lines that only trail off, like the yellow mage's hand signs, no longer make it guard.
- **It keeps exploring.** When nothing new is one door away, it walks on to the nearest place it has not seen. A wait it chose itself ends after two minutes.
- **Close to death with nothing to heal, it stops exploring alone** and comes back to you, and says so.
- **When the player it follows dies, it stays with the other player** until they are called back.
- **The 0.1.13 known issues are fixed.** After a wait it goes back to the player who said "wait". A slow answer to an older order no longer undoes a newer one. When the game itself walks it onto a trap tile, it no longer stops there forever.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
