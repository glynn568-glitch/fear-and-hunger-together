**Download `FearAndHunger-Together-0.1.21.zip` below**, unzip it anywhere, and double-click **Fear & Hunger Together**. Everyone in a game needs the same version.

Fixed:

- **Instanced loot now covers bodies.** Searching a guard, a priest or most other bodies gives each of you your own loot, as with crates. Skinning a body still uses it up for both of you. Some coin-flip chests were missed too; they're covered now. The 0.1.20 notes listed lockpick chests as a known issue, but those already worked.
- **Both of you searching the same body at once** could give you both its loot. With Instanced loot off, the first to search it takes it.
- **Following your partner through some doors** could leave you with the wrong walls on the other side.

Known issues:

- If you split up across two floors of one area, an enemy your partner wakes on their floor can wake on yours too.

Found a bug? Press Ctrl+Shift+B in the game and paste what it copies into a new issue.
